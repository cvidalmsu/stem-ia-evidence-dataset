# Limitations and safeguards

- The profiles are exhaustive over a declared state space, not representative
  of the frequency or complexity of classroom events.
- The rule specification and generated oracle share the same design source;
  internal agreement cannot establish external validity.
- The labels do not diagnose a learner or explain every reason for an
  unsuccessful task.
- The actions are candidate supports, not validated interventions.
- The curriculum-domain mapping is a design mapping pending independent expert
  review and precise learning-objective validation.
- No automated Scratch, Tinkercad, Arduino, image, code, or natural-language
  evidence extraction is included.
- No usability, teacher workload, explanation comprehension, fairness, or
  learning-outcome claim can be inferred.
- No participant records are included. Any future participant-level extension
  requires ethics approval, consent/assent, data minimization, disclosure-risk
  review, governance documentation, and a separate versioned release.
